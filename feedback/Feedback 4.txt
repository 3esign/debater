Cut it. The wager amounts shown in the Ledger Terminal can be hardcoded to the genesis script and simulated for live matches without tracking balances.
One new concern: Canvas-confetti on wager payouts implies users are tracking real winnings. Combined with mock USDC, you're building the UI language of a gambling product without the actual mechanics. That's either confusing (users think they won something) or meaningless (users know it's fake and don't care). Decide: is this a game with fake points, or a simulator that illustrates what a real contract would do? The current plan is neither clearly, which is the worst outcome. The "simulator" framing is stronger — lean into it.
Net verdict: This is a shippable plan if you make three changes before writing a line of code:

Add /debate/[id] routing now, not later
Decide "game vs simulator" and remove the ambiguous mock-USDC/confetti if you go simulator
Pick two LLM providers for launch and stub the others with a "coming soon" notice in the KeysModal

The bones are solid. Don't let the theming work start before the routing story is settled — that's the trap every cyberpunk UI project falls into.
